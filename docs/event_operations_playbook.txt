================================================================================
EVENTOPS AI — ENTERPRISE EVENT OPERATIONS PLAYBOOK (GENERAL REFERENCE GUIDE)
================================================================================
NOTICE: This document provides general, practical event operations guidance and
operational best practices for executive dinners, salons, and professional
gatherings. It is designed as an operational knowledge base and does not claim to
represent the proprietary internal policies of any specific enterprise or vendor.
================================================================================

1. EVENT LIFECYCLE & MILESTONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- T-6 Weeks (Vision & Format Lock):
  * Define event objective, target audience profile, guest count ceiling, and financial ceiling.
  * Establish protected priorities (e.g. culinary quality, intimate acoustic environment, accessibility).
- T-4 Weeks (Venue Selection & Budget Baseline):
  * Inspect venue for physical accessibility, elevator access, door closure, and ambient acoustics (< 65 dB).
  * Lock venue contract with explicit Food & Beverage minimum and cancellation/rescheduling terms.
  * Establish baseline budget with 8-15% unallocated contingency reserve.
- T-3 Weeks (Invitations & Inclusivity Survey):
  * Send invitations with clear framing, dress code, schedule, and dietary intake survey.
  * Survey must inquire about mobility accommodations, dietary allergies/preferences, and zero-proof beverage options.
- T-2 Weeks (Program & Run of Show Draft):
  * Sequence minute-by-minute Run of Show (ROS) with designated owners for each transition.
  * Ensure speeches or toasts do not exceed 5-7 minutes during dining to preserve food temperature and conversational flow.
- T-72 Hours (Operational & Dietary Lock):
  * Confirm final guest headcount and dietary list with venue culinary team.
  * Require the kitchen to hold at least 2 reserve allergen-free / vegan plates for unanticipated guest needs.
  * Re-confirm arrival staff: dedicated greeter and coat check attendant for events with 20+ guests.
- Day of Event (T-0):
  * T-90 min: Event Lead on-site for room inspection, table spacing check (> 36" clearance), lighting level check, and acoustic test.
  * T-15 min: Welcome drinks and ambient music active.
  * Post-Event (T+24h): Send personalized thank you, opt-in attendee roster, and conduct financial reconciliation.

2. ACCESSIBILITY & UNIVERSAL DESIGN STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Physical Access:
  * Entrances must feature step-free access or functional ADA-compliant lifts.
  * Restrooms must be on the same level as the private event space or accessible via direct elevator.
  * Aisle clearance between tables and behind seated chairs must maintain at least 36 inches to comfortably accommodate wheelchairs and mobility devices.
- Sensory & Acoustic Comfort:
  * Executive dinners must maintain conversational acoustic levels. Background music should be gentle (jazz, acoustic instrumental, or ambient downtempo) and never drown out natural speaking volume.
  * Lighting must be warm (2200K - 2700K color temperature), dimmable, and free of harsh fluorescent glare or flickering bulbs.
  * Provide or identify a quiet decompression space nearby for guests who may experience sensory fatigue.
- Beverage Inclusivity:
  * Non-alcoholic options must never be an afterthought. Offer artisanal zero-proof elixirs, botanical sodas, or crafted mocktails served in identical luxury glassware to alcoholic pairings.

3. DIETARY MANAGEMENT & ALLERGEN PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Clear Labeling: Plated courses must be cross-checked against individual seat assignments or discrete dietary service markers.
- Kitchen Reserves: Always arrange for 2-3 emergency vegan/gluten-free/nut-free entrée plates even if all RSVPs indicated standard diets.
- Cross-Contamination: For severe anaphylactic allergies (peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish), mandate strict kitchen separation protocols in the venue event agreement.

4. BUDGET RECONCILIATION & LOCAL TAX/GRATUITY NORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Metropolitan (NYC) Dining Realities:
  * Always calculate base food and beverage costs separately from local sales tax (NYC: 8.875%), administrative fees (typically 3-5%), and mandatory gratuities (typically 20%).
  * A nominal $100/head menu actually incurs ~$133.65/head in gross commitment after tax, admin fee, and gratuity.
- Contingency Reserve Discipline:
  * Never allocate 100% of the total budget to planned line items. Maintain a strict 8% to 15% contingency reserve.
  * Contingency funds are dedicated to last-minute headcount variations, overtime charges, urgent floral adjustments, or transportation assistance.

5. RUN OF SHOW (ROS) PRINCIPLES & CUEING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Arrival Window: Allocate a minimum of 25-30 minutes for arrival, decompression, coat check, and initial welcome beverage before attempting to seat guests.
- Transition Cues: Never shout over a room. Transition cues should be subtle: a slight lowering of background music, service staff opening dining doors, or the host tapping a glass gently.
- Table Discussion Prompts: Keep prompts open-ended, non-judgmental, and focused on shared learning rather than corporate elevator pitches.
- Course Pacing: Allow 35-45 minutes per course. Do not clear plates until at least 80% of the table has completed their dish.

6. DATA MINIMIZATION & GUEST PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Collect only operationally necessary guest data (name, email, dietary/accessibility needs, title/organization).
- Never share guest phone numbers or direct contact details without explicit, opt-in consent.
- Post-event attendee rosters should be opt-in only.
- In-room photography should respect guests' privacy preferences, with discrete badges or seating indicators for those who prefer not to appear in social media photography.

7. OPERATIONAL READINESS BENCHMARKS (EVENTOPS GUARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Readiness Score >= 85: Ready for Execution. All critical logistics, staffing, dietary locks, and budget reconciliations are satisfied.
- Readiness Score 65 - 84: Needs Attention. Minor gaps in contingency reserves, vendor contracts, or acoustic verifications require timely resolution.
- Readiness Score < 65: High Risk. Immediate operational blockers (budget deficit, missing coat check for large gathering, unassigned key roles) must be resolved prior to proceeding.
